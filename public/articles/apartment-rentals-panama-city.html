A Panama City, Panama apartment rental costs about $90 to $105 a night for a typical short-stay listing, or roughly $800 to $1,500 a month on a long-term lease, depending on neighborhood and size, as of November 2026. Casco Viejo is the walkable, historic choice, with top-rated one-bedrooms at about $85 to $165 a night. Avenida Balboa, Calle 50 and Obarrio offer modern towers with pools near the Metro. For any stay under 45 days, first confirm the building allows short stays, because the law in the District of Panama reserves them for properties with a tourist-lodging permit.

Searches for a Panama City apartment rental return a mix of Airbnb pages, agency listings and, confusingly, condos in Panama City, Florida. This guide covers only Panama City, Panama. It explains which neighborhoods to rent in, what nightly and monthly rentals cost in late 2026, the building rules that can sink a short stay, and the lease law that applies once you stay longer.

If you are still choosing between an apartment and a hotel, our guide to where to stay in Panama City compares the areas and the best hotels in each.

Good to know · FLORIDA MIX-UP

Several rental sites and search summaries mix Panama City, Panama with Panama City Beach, Florida. If a listing quotes Gulf-front condos, Pier Park or St. Andrews Bay, it is in Florida. Check the map pin before you compare prices.

Prices

Panama City Apartment Rental: Areas and Prices for 2026

A typical short-stay apartment in Panama City costs about $90 to $105 a night. A long-term lease runs roughly $11.60 to $15 per square meter per month, depending on the neighborhood. Airbtics puts the city's median daily rate at B/.90 across 2,981 tracked listings for September 2025 to September 2026 (the balboa trades at par with the US dollar).[1] AirROI's figure for the Panamá district is a median of about $105 a night, with a higher average of $140.[2]

Prices are flat rather than climbing. AirDNA counts 3,881 active listings in Panamá province as of September 2026, with an average nightly rate of $93 that is down 0.7% from a year earlier.[3] The "from $10 a night" figure on Airbnb's Panama City pages is a marketplace floor before taxes and fees, mostly rooms and outliers. It is not what a whole apartment costs.[4]

Which Area Fits Which Stay

Avenida Balboa and the Cinta Costera are the classic short-stay strip: towers facing the bay, with the waterfront promenade at the door. A listing at The Sands describes the building as steps from the Cinta Costera, with an infinity pool.[4] At YOO Panama, a 54th-floor, 134 m² one-bedroom comes with a building pool, gym, three restaurants and a mini-market.[8] Downtown listings on this side put Casco Viejo about 15 minutes away on foot and the 5 de Mayo Metro station about 5 minutes away.[8]

Calle 50, Marbella and Obarrio suit people who work from the apartment. One listing between Marbella and Obarrio, near the Tornillo building, is walkable to the Metro and the Cinta Costera.[8] PH The Gray on Calle 50 is a newer building with a rooftop pool, gym and a coworking space.[4] A Bella Vista listing on Calle 50 puts the Cinta Costera 10 minutes away on foot.[9]

El Cangrejo and San Francisco are the cheaper central options, with fewer amenity-heavy towers and more local life. One El Cangrejo listing puts Casco Viejo, the canal locks, the Causeway and the main malls within a 10-minute drive.[9] Older buildings here can be awkward for families: one El Cangrejo unit in a 1950s two-story building states it is not suitable for children under 10.[10]

Punta Paitilla, Punta Pacífica, Coco del Mar and Costa del Este run east along the water, with bigger units and higher rents. They suit families and groups. One 6-bedroom house in Punta Paitilla with a private pool sleeps up to 17.[8] A Coco del Mar listing advertises 100 MB internet and closeness to the airport.[8]

Amador and Ancón are the quiet, green fringe. A Causeway listing advertises views of ships passing into the canal, with the Biomuseo within walking distance.[9] If that appeals, our Amador Causeway and Biomuseo guide covers what is out there. In Ancón, small studios sit near Cerro Ancón and the Albrook Metro station.[10]

Airbnb

Airbnb in Panama City: Cheap, Best-Rated and the Real Total

Most short-stay apartments in Panama City are listed on Airbnb. The Tourism Authority (ATP) counts about 13,000 Airbnb units in Panama, against roughly 25,000 hotel rooms.[11] That depth means you can find both cheap studios and designer penthouses. It also means ratings run high everywhere: the city average is 4.7 out of 5.[4] A 4.7 is therefore an ordinary score in Panama City, not a sign of a standout stay.

Cheap Airbnb in Panama City

The cheapest whole apartments are usually outside the Avenida Balboa strip and Casco Viejo. Look in El Cangrejo, San Francisco, Bella Vista and around Albrook, where budget travelers report basic whole apartments advertised from around $40 a night. Being near the Metro matters more than being near the water when you are on a budget. Casita Curundu, for example, sits next to the Metropolitan Natural Park and close to Albrook Mall, the bus terminal and the Metro.[10]

Book at least three nights where you can, because a flat cleaning fee weighs heaviest on one- and two-night stays. Read the house rules on cheaper listings closely. Some set a 3:00 p.m. check-in and ban parties and smoking indoors.[10]

Best-rated Airbnb Buildings and Listings

Guest ratings matter most when they sit on hundreds of reviews. A two-bedroom, 2.5-bath ocean-view unit in the YOO tower on Avenida Balboa holds a 4.9 from 339 reviews.[12] The Casco Viejo designer penthouse with a watchtower bedroom holds a 4.86 from 415 reviews.[4] For full-service buildings, one Cinta Costera one-bedroom lists 24/7 security, a gym, pools, four restaurants, a bar and a sky lounge in the tower.[9]

What You Actually Pay: Fees and Taxes

Most Airbnb hosts now pay a single 15.5% service fee out of their payout, so guests on those listings see no separate Airbnb service charge at checkout.[13] Roughly 94% of listings in the Panamá district are on this model.[2] The price you see before taxes is close to the total. On three nights at $150 with a $60 cleaning fee, for example, the guest pays $510.[14]

Cleaning fees vary widely: the median is $35, but the average is $79 because a minority of listings charge much more.[2] Always compare the all-in figure, not the nightly rate.

Tax is unsettled. AirROI's data shows lodging taxes added to the guest's bill, but whether and how ITBMS (Panama's sales tax) applies to short rentals is still being decided in the Assembly.[2] One pending bill sets a 10% rate.[15] Another proposes 15%.[16] Short-stay rentals are rarely invoiced today. If you need a receipt for business expenses, ask the host before booking.[15]

Casco Viejo

Casco Viejo Apartments and Airbnbs: What You Get and the Trade-Offs

Casco Viejo is the best-located area in the city if you want to walk to restaurants, plazas and rooftop bars. It is a poor fit if you need quiet weekend nights, a car or an elevator. The district was founded in 1673, after the pirate Henry Morgan destroyed Panamá Viejo.[17] UNESCO added it to the World Heritage List in 1997. The national heritage office (DNPH) oversees its nearly one thousand buildings, which limits what owners can change.[18]

What Casco Viejo Rentals Look Like

Expect restored colonial buildings more than towers. Listings include a one-bedroom loft in a restored 1850 building with original calicanto stone walls.[4] There is also a renovated 95 m² apartment with a balcony overlooking Hotel Central, which asks guests to inquire about parking.[19] The best-known listing is a three-story designer penthouse with what it calls the country's oldest surviving watchtower.[4] Some booking sites date the house, Casa Marqués de Portago next to the Presidential Palace, to 1573.[20] That date appears in rental listings, not in official heritage records.

Food is part of the draw. One listing points out that Fonda Lo Que Hay, a short walk away, is ranked among Latin America's top 50 culinary destinations.[4] Our Casco Viejo walking route maps the main stops if you want to see what is around a listing before you choose it.

Casco Viejo Airbnb Prices

Top-rated one-bedroom apartments in Casco Viejo charged about B/.85 to B/.165 a night between September 2025 and September 2026, at occupancy of 80% to 85% or more.[1] Those are the best-reviewed units, not the cheapest. High occupancy also means the good ones fill up, so book well ahead for dry-season and holiday dates. If you would rather have a front desk and daily cleaning, our comparison of the best boutique hotels in Casco Viejo covers prices there.

Noise, Stairs and Parking

Weekend nightlife is the main complaint. Under a December 2024 decree, the Casco Antiguo became a special nightlife zone, where permitted bars could operate without fixed closing hours.[21] An August 2026 decree revised the district's bar hours again. It named Friday and Saturday as the busiest nightlife nights in these zones.[22] On pedestrian-only "Casco Peatonal" days, sound equipment above permitted levels is banned, with fines of B/.50 to B/.300.[23]

Buildings are old, so many have steep stairs and no elevator. Few have on-site parking, and street parking is tight. Ask before booking if you are traveling with heavy bags, small children or a rental car. For the after-dark picture by neighborhood, see our guide to whether Panama is safe.

Good to know · CASCO NOISE

Ask the host which street the bedroom faces and how far the unit is from the nearest bar or rooftop. An upper-floor unit on a side street is usually quieter than one above a plaza.

Rules

Short-term Rental Rules in Panama City Buildings

In the District of Panama, renting an apartment for less than 45 days is legally reserved for properties with a tourist-lodging permit from the ATP. Many condo buildings also set their own limits through their bylaws. Article 21 of Ley 80 of 2012 prohibits rentals of under 45 days in the district to anyone without that permit. It also lets the ATP fine both the person renting the unit and anyone advertising it, including online.[24]

How the 45-day Rule Is Enforced

Enforcement mostly starts with complaints from buildings. In June 2025, the ATP admitted a complaint from the condo board of the Yacht Club building against the company renting out an apartment there for stays under 45 days.[24] Ley 80 fines run up to $50,000, according to reporting on the bill that would repeal them.[15] Yet the ATP's administrator said in 2026 that Airbnb-type rentals still have no tourism-specific regulation or tax. She added that no national registry records where these units are or who runs them.[25]

The Building's Own Rules

Most apartment towers are run under Panama's horizontal-property law, Ley 284 of 2022.[26] Each building has a reglamento de copropiedad (condo bylaws), and a Panamanian law firm says these bylaws can regulate rentals through platforms like Airbnb.[27] Owners must make tenants and visitors follow the law and the bylaws. They must also give the board a copy of the ID or passport of anyone they rent to.[28] So expect the front desk to ask for your passport. Some Panamanian property consultants say many buildings ban rentals shorter than six months outright, with per-incident fines.

The practical risk lands on you as the guest. In a building that bans short stays, a booking can be cancelled at the last minute, or security can refuse entry. Under Ley 284, a breach of the bylaws or well-founded complaints from neighbors can lead the board to revoke an owner's authorization immediately.[28]

What Is Changing in 2026

Two bills would change the rules, and neither is law as of November 2026. PL 301 passed its first debate on January 6, 2026. It would legalize tourist rentals of 1 to 90 days, repeal the Ley 80 fines and set a 10% ITBMS, the same rate hotels pay.[15] Bill 31-26, presented on September 14, 2026, proposes 15% ITBMS on short tourist rentals, and it still needs three debates.[16] The ATP has said that paying the tax would not legalize rentals that local rules prohibit.[11]

Long Stays

Monthly Rentals and Long-Term Leases: Deposits, Contracts and Stay Limits

For stays of a month or more, a lease costs far less than booking night by night. A month at the $90 median comes to about $2,700, against roughly $800 to $1,100 for a 70 m² one-bedroom on a lease. Rentals of more than 45 days also fall outside Ley 80's short-stay rule.[15] Many hosts list weekly and monthly rates on Airbnb, so ask for a long-stay price even on nightly listings.

Agencies advise renting before buying, so you can test a neighborhood's noise, walkability and building management first.[6] If buying is the longer-term plan, our guide to Panama condos for sale to foreigners covers areas, prices and building fees. For a fuller picture of monthly budgets, see living in Panama as an expat.

What Panama's Lease Law Says

Residential leases are governed by Ley 93 of 1973, the rental law, rather than the Civil Code. Some of its rules protect tenants more than many landlords' standard contracts suggest.

Deposit
One month's rent. It is returned at the end of the lease unless there is unpaid rent or damage beyond normal wear, verified before the rental authority.[29]
Who holds the deposit
Not the landlord directly. The 1974 text names the housing ministry's rental office (Dirección General de Arrendamientos).[30] The consolidated text names a separate account at Banco Nacional or Caja de Ahorros.[29]
Advance rent
Rent is paid monthly, at the end of each month. A contract cannot oblige you to pay in advance. If you choose to prepay, the law entitles you to a discount of at least 10%.[29]
Written contract
Every residential lease must be in writing on the rental office's forms. It must state names and ID numbers, the property, the rent, the term and the use. The landlord must file a copy within five business days.[29]
Term and leaving early
On paper, no lease may run under three years, and a tenant who is current on rent may renew. The term binds the landlord, but you can leave with 30 days' written notice. Without notice, you can lose part or all of the deposit.[29]
Notary
The Public Registry's manual says leases should be notarized.[31] The Civil Code requires a public deed only for leases of six years or more that must bind third parties.[32]
If the owner sells
A buyer must respect existing leases made by the seller.[29]
Good to know · DEPOSITS

If a landlord asks for several months up front, remember that the lease law bars contracts from requiring advance rent. A deposit equal to one month is the legal standard. Get a receipt for every payment.

How Long You Can Stay as a Tourist

Your stay limit depends on your passport, not your lease. Nationals who don't need a visa can stay as tourists for up to 3 months from entry. Those who need a visa generally get 1 month.[33] Citizens of the United States and Canada receive up to 180 days.[34] At entry, you may be asked for a passport valid for at least 3 months, a return ticket and proof of at least US$500 in funds.[35]

Long-stay visitors are common. In the 2018 tourism survey, visitors staying more than 15 days in rented housing averaged 62.6 days.[36] Renting an apartment does not extend your permitted stay. Our guide to Panama residency visas covers the options for living there legally.

Good to know · STAY LIMITS

Your tourist days count from the moment you enter Panama. Match the end date of a monthly lease or booking to your permitted stay rather than to a round number of months.

Checklist

What to Check Before You Book an Apartment in Panama City

The checks that matter most are whether the building allows your length of stay, what the all-in price is, and, for leases, whether the contract follows Ley 93. Work through them in this order, from the cheapest mistake to catch to the most expensive.

Pre-booking Checklist

1

Confirm the city and the map pin 2 min

Make sure the listing is in Panama City, Panama, not Florida. Check that the pin matches the neighborhood named in the title.

2

Ask whether the building allows your stay length 1 message

For stays under 45 days, ask whether the unit has an ATP tourist-lodging permit and whether the building's bylaws allow short rentals. A vague answer is a warning sign.

3

Pin down the exact location in Casco Viejo 1 message

Ask which street the bedroom faces, the floor, and whether there is an elevator or parking. These decide your sleep on Friday and Saturday nights.

4

Test the internet if you work remotely 5 min

Ask the host for a recent speed-test screenshot rather than relying on a "fast wifi" label. Ask whether the building has a coworking room as a backup.

5

Compare the all-in price 5 min

Add the cleaning fee and any taxes to the nightly rate. For a month or longer, compare that total with a monthly or agency rate in the same area.

6

Read reviews for building access 10 min

Look for mentions of check-in at the front desk, passport registration, and pool or gym rules for short-term guests. Prefer listings with hundreds of reviews over a perfect score on a handful.

7

For a lease, check the contract and deposit Before signing

Insist on a written contract with your passport number, the rent, the term and the use. The deposit should be one month, with no forced advance rent. Ask which utilities, internet and building fees are included, since air conditioning drives the power bill.

Questions

Before you go

Is Airbnb legal in Panama City, Panama?

Only partly: rentals under 45 days need an ATP tourist-lodging permit. Ley 80 of 2012 sets this rule for the District of Panama and lets the ATP fine hosts and advertisers. Many buildings add their own bans in their bylaws. Two bills that would formally regulate and tax these rentals were still pending in the Assembly as of November 2026.

How much does an Airbnb in Casco Viejo cost per night?

Top-rated one-bedroom apartments in Casco Viejo ran about $85 to $165 a night between September 2025 and September 2026. Those are the best-reviewed units, which stay 80% or more booked, so reserve early for dry-season dates. Add the cleaning fee before comparing listings.

How much does it cost to rent an apartment in Panama City per month?

Roughly $800 to $1,100 for a 70 m² one-bedroom and $1,150 to $1,500 for a 100 m² two-bedroom, based on July 2026 median rents per square meter. San Francisco and El Cangrejo sit at the low end and Costa del Este at the top. Furnished and new-building units usually cost more.

Is it cheaper to rent monthly than to book an Airbnb for a month?

Yes, usually by more than half. A month at the city's $90 median nightly rate comes to about $2,700, while a one-bedroom lease in a central area runs around $800 to $1,100. Ask Airbnb hosts for a monthly rate if you want to avoid a lease.

What is the deposit for renting an apartment in Panama?

One month's rent, under Panama's rental law (Ley 93 of 1973). The law also says a contract cannot oblige you to pay rent in advance. You can end a lease early with 30 days' written notice without losing the deposit.

Is Casco Viejo noisy on weekends?

Often, yes, especially on Friday and Saturday nights. The district has been treated as a special nightlife zone, and permitted bars there have operated with extended hours. Choose an upper-floor unit on a side street and ask which way the bedroom faces. See our Casco Viejo walking route to spot the busiest plazas.

Are there taxes on Airbnb stays in Panama?

The tax treatment is still being settled. One bill would apply 10% ITBMS to short tourist rentals and another proposes 15%, and neither had passed as of November 2026. Short rentals are rarely invoiced today, so ask the host before booking if you need a receipt.

How long can I stay in Panama on a tourist entry while renting an apartment?

Up to 3 months for most visa-exempt nationalities, and up to 180 days for US and Canadian citizens. Nationals who need a visa generally get 1 month. A lease does not extend your stay; for that, see our guide to Panama residency visas.

Does Casco Viejo have parking?

Very little. Most buildings are restored colonial houses without garages, and some listings offer parking only on request. If you plan to drive, ask before booking, or stay on Avenida Balboa or Calle 50, where towers have garages.

Which area of Panama City is best for digital nomads?

Calle 50, Obarrio and Marbella suit most remote workers, with Metro access and newer towers that include coworking rooms and gyms. Avenida Balboa adds the Cinta Costera waterfront. Our guide to where to stay in Panama City compares the areas in more detail.
