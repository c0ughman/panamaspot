The cost of stem cell treatment in Panama is about $10,000 to $30,000 or more for an adult protocol as of November 2026. Children's protocols start at about $14,500 at the clinics that publish prices. You pay out of pocket before treatment. The price usually covers the cells, the infusions and the clinic's own tests, while flights, meals and often the hotel are extra.

The cost of stem cell treatment in Panama comes down to three things: the clinic, the protocol, and what the package bundles. Most Panama City clinics use donor mesenchymal stem cells (MSCs) from umbilical cord tissue, given over several days, mostly by IV. Only a few clinics put a dollar figure on their own websites.

This page covers the money: published prices, what they include, how Panama compares with the US and Mexico, how payment and insurance work, and the full trip budget. Our guide to stem cell therapy in Panama covers which clinics operate and how a treatment week runs. None of the prices below say anything about whether a protocol works. The regulation and evidence section explains what Panamanian and US regulators and published studies actually say.

Prices

Stem Cell Treatment Cost in Panama, Clinic by Clinic

Published prices in Panama run from about $10,000 to $30,000 or more for an adult protocol as of November 2026. Only two clinics state their numbers on their own sites.[1][2] The rest quote after you apply, so the figures you see repeated online often trace back to third-party listings rather than the clinic.

What Each Clinic Publishes

Auragens occupies the 48th floor of Oceania Business Plaza. It lists treatment from $14,500 for children 12 and under and from $28,750 for adults, with prices rising based on the protocol its medical staff design.[2] No upper limit is published, so the adult figure is a floor, not a typical price.

Regeneration Clinic of Panama says treatment costs $10,000 to $30,000 depending on the protocol.[1] Google's AI summary quotes the same clinic at $5,000 to $30,000. The clinic's own FAQ puts the floor at $10,000, so that is the safer starting figure. Its protocols run 3 to 7 days with one to five applications of MSCs.[3]

Stem Cell Institute shares the BICSA Financial Center with the Hilton Panama and does not post a price list. Its site says only that its concierge team helps arrange your stay.[4] Broker sites repeat starting prices of about $15,825 for children and $26,900 for adults. A US clinic's review of Panama says adult protocols at the leading clinic start near $26,900, and that patients report figures like $22,000 for a single condition.[5] Treat these numbers as indications until you have a written quote. Our Stem Cell Institute Panama review covers its pricing, patient complaints and alternatives in detail.

PCT Panama uses fat-derived (adipose) MSCs rather than cord tissue. It gives an individualized estimate only after it reviews your medical history.[6] BioGenesis, inside the JW Marriott Panama, offers Wharton's jelly MSC therapy and describes its pricing only as a fraction of US costs.[7]

Prices by Type of Treatment

Panama clinics price by protocol rather than by condition, but the pattern is consistent. As a general guide, a single joint injection with a patient's own cells may cost a few thousand dollars, while multi-day neurological or systemic programs often run $20,000 to $30,000 or more.[8]

  • Knees, shoulders, hips and back. At Regeneration Clinic, a specific joint may get an intra-articular injection from an orthopedist on top of the IV.[1] Ask whether that joint shot is part of the protocol price or a separate line. Even a US clinic that writes about Panama notes that the case for an international trip is much weaker for orthopedic problems, because similar regenerative care is available at home.[5]
  • Systemic, autoimmune and neurological protocols. These are the multi-infusion packages that sit at the top of the range: Auragens' adult protocols start at $28,750, and Regeneration Clinic's run to $30,000.[2][1]
  • Children, including autism protocols. Auragens starts at $14,500 for children 12 and under.[2] A 2019 description of Stem Cell Institute's five-day autism package put it at about $13,000 to $18,000 depending on the child's weight, with a Hilton room included. That figure is several years old, so treat it as history, not a current price. Our page on stem cell therapy for autism and MS in Panama covers what clinics offer for those conditions and what the research shows.

Why the Published Ranges Are So Wide

A $20,000 spread inside one clinic usually comes from a handful of variables. Cell numbers are customized and, in some cases, set by body weight.[1] The delivery route matters too: Auragens lists IV infusion, ultrasound-guided intramuscular injection, intra-articular injection and intrathecal injection, which goes into the spinal fluid.[2] Each added route or session raises the price. Optional extras such as hyperbaric oxygen, red light therapy, PEMF and vitamin IVs can be layered on as well.[2]

Good to know · STARTING PRICES

"From $28,750" is the cheapest adult protocol, not the average one. When you compare clinics, compare written quotes for the same plan: the same number of sessions, the same routes and the same extras.

Inclusions

What's Included in a Panama Stem Cell Package, and What Isn't

The treatment price usually covers the cells, the infusions and the clinic's own tests. International flights, meals and extra procedures are usually on top, and lodging depends on the clinic. PCT Panama says flights and hotels are generally not part of the treatment cost, though some clinics have hotel partnerships.[6]

Cells and infusions
Always in the protocol price. Stem Cell Institute says most patients receive at least three infusions. Each IV session takes 20 to 30 minutes and needs no anesthesia.[9]
Bloodwork and evaluation
Regeneration Clinic includes bloodwork, plus a medical evaluation and a vitamin or Meyer's Cocktail IV.[1] Its wider assessment menu includes body composition, gut health, DNA methylation and EEG.[3] Ask which of those tests are in your price.
Airport transfers
Included at Regeneration Clinic.[1] Stem Cell Institute's concierge meets patients at the gate at Tocumen International Airport, takes them through customs, and drives them to the Hilton Panama.[9]
Hotel
Stem Cell Institute includes a standard Hilton Panama room in the cost of treatment, one elevator ride from the clinic.[9] Elsewhere, assume you pay for lodging unless the quote says otherwise.
Joint and spine injections
Often a separate line from the IV protocol. Confirm in writing.
Wellness add-ons
Hyperbaric oxygen, red light, cryotherapy, massage and coaching are listed by Auragens as optional enhancements.[2]
Follow-up
Clinics describe aftercare as remote contact and a home plan, often with supplements.[3] Lab work or imaging back home is usually your own cost.

How a Quote Turns into a Final Price

The process at most Panama clinics follows the same sequence. The price can still move at step five, after you have already traveled.

1

Application and records Before travel

You send your medical history, privacy forms and any imaging or bloodwork the clinic asks for. At Auragens, a candidacy review requires no commitment.[2]

2

Protocol proposal Before travel

The medical team reviews your application and proposes a protocol, which sets the cell numbers and sessions.[1]

3

Written estimate Before travel

The clinic issues a cost estimate. Get it itemized: cells, sessions, routes, tests, transfers, lodging and add-ons.

4

Payment Before treatment

At Auragens, payment is arranged before service.[2]

5

On-site evaluation Day one

If the exam turns up new information or calls for extra procedures, the estimate may be adjusted.[6]

6

Treatment days 3 to 7 days

Appointments at Regeneration Clinic take about an hour each, and patients go about their day afterward.[1]

Good to know · QUOTE CHANGES

Before you pay, ask the clinic to state in writing what happens if the day-one evaluation changes the plan or rules you out. Ask what extra items would cost and whether any of the payment is refunded.

Comparison

Panama vs the US vs Mexico: Stem Cell Prices Compared

Panama sits at the top of the Latin American price range, and a Panama protocol often costs more than a single US injection. The two are usually different products, though, so a straight dollar comparison misleads. A US clinic reviewing Panama puts it at the higher end of the international market, commonly $10,000 to $30,000 or more, and notably pricier than Mexico or Costa Rica.[5]

The US: Cheaper per Shot, Not the Same Product

US prices are usually quoted per injection or per treated area. National ranges run about $3,000 to $10,000 for a single stem cell injection, $4,000 to $10,000 per area for umbilical cord tissue (Wharton's jelly) products, and $3,000 to $7,000 for bone marrow concentrate.[10] One Miami clinic gives planning ranges of $4,000 to $7,000 for a single injection and $8,000 to $17,000 for autism IV therapy.[11] A Utah and Nevada clinic charges $1,999 for one MSC IV or one joint injection, and claims most US clinics charge $8,000 to $25,000 per treatment.[12]

A fully itemized US quote can add up quickly. One cost guide lists consultation and imaging at $800 to $2,500, cell harvest at $5,000 to $12,000, administration at $3,500 to $9,000 per site, and a follow-up package at $600 to $1,500.[8]

The catch is the product, not just the price. The high-dose, lab-expanded donor MSC infusions that Panama clinics sell are not legally marketed in the US outside FDA-overseen trials. The FDA treats umbilical cord products that are more than minimally manipulated, or used for purposes other than their original function, as drugs and biologics that need premarket approval. It sent warning letters on this to three umbilical cord product companies in 2025.[13][14][15] A cheap US umbilical cord shot and a Panama IV protocol are therefore not two prices for the same thing.

Mexico: The Widest Range, Often the Lowest Floor

Mexico's prices start lower and spread wider than Panama's. Giostar in Cancún lists protocols from $6,900 to $18,968.[16] Stemaid in Los Cabos quoted $1,000 to $1,500 per injection in 2025. Its protocol runs 12 to 24 injections over two to four weeks, so the total and the length of stay grow together.[17] Broker surveys put Mexico anywhere from about $1,500 to $30,000. That spread reflects very different cell types and protocols, not one market price.

Brokers usually name Mexico, Turkey and Thailand as the cheapest destinations. Cheapest is a poor filter on its own. A US clinic's pricing guide warns that unusually low prices are a red flag when they come with unclear cell sourcing, vague claims or a thin medical evaluation.[11]

What Panama's Premium Pays for

Part of Panama's higher price is logistics. Packages bundle airport concierge, transfers and, at one clinic, a hotel room in the same building. Panama is about a three-hour flight from Miami, uses the US dollar, and requires no visa for US and Canadian citizens.[5] Panama City also has accredited private hospitals, including one affiliated with Johns Hopkins Medicine International, in case something goes wrong during your stay.[5] Our guide to healthcare in Panama for foreigners covers those hospitals and their costs.

The Rules

What the Price Buys: Regulation and Evidence in 2026

Whatever you pay, you are buying a treatment that Panama's own rules class as experimental for nearly every condition clinics list. La Prensa reports that under Executive Decree 179 of June 2018, the only stem cell use with recognized clinical evidence in Panama is hematopoietic (blood-forming) transplantation. Any other use may only be done within protocols approved by the National Bioethics Committee for Research (CNBI) and MINSA's National Directorate of Pharmacy and Drugs.[18] The same report found that none of the commercial stem cell clinics it identified had submitted research protocols to the bioethics committee since 2018.[18]

Clinic marketing frames this differently. Some marketing pages say stem cell therapy is simply legal in Panama, and a US clinic says Panama's leading operations run protocols approved by an institutional review board.[19][5] If a clinic says its protocol is approved, ask for the committee's name and the approval number. The CNBI sits under MINSA, was created by Executive Decree 1843 of 2014, is regulated by Law 84 of 2019, and lists 18 accredited committees.[20]

On the US side, the FDA's consumer page says the only approved stem cell products are blood-forming cells from umbilical cord blood, for disorders of blood production.[21] In December 2024, the FDA also approved Ryoncil (remestemcel-L), an MSC product, for a single use: children with steroid-refractory acute graft-versus-host disease. The FDA adds that if you are offered these products outside a clinical trial it oversees, you are likely being offered an illegally marketed product. It also says that FDA registration of a lab does not make a product legally marketed.[21] Auragens says the same about its own FDA-registered lab: registration covers the facility, not any therapy.[2]

The published research is thin for the price. Auragens describes its therapies as investigational for most conditions, with safety and effectiveness not established through controlled trials.[2] A 2025 phase 1b trial of IV Wharton's jelly MSCs in acute graft-versus-host disease treated 24 patients and had no control arm.[22] A Malaysian registry study of IV umbilical cord MSCs in type 2 diabetes was retrospective, and its authors say a randomized controlled trial is still needed.[23]

Paying

Insurance, HSA/FSA and How You Pay for Stem Cell Therapy in Panama

No health insurer pays for these protocols, so plan on paying the full amount yourself. Regeneration Clinic says its treatments aren't covered by local or international insurance.[1] Auragens likewise says treatment is not covered.[2]

Health Insurance and Medicare

Insurers generally classify regenerative stem cell therapies as investigational and limit coverage to approved clinical trials.[8] Medicare's national coverage determination 110.23 covers stem cell transplantation, meaning cells harvested from bone marrow or blood and infused to restore blood-forming function.[24] Where no national policy exists, regional Medicare contractors decide coverage under the "reasonable and necessary" standard.[25] Medicare does not cover stem cell injections for joint conditions.[10]

One partial exception helps at home. Some plans cover related diagnostics or doctor visits while excluding the procedure itself.[11] Pre-trip imaging, bloodwork and follow-up visits with your own doctor may therefore be billable to your insurer, so ask before you book them.

HSA and FSA Funds

Some US clinics say stem cell therapy is generally a qualified medical expense for HSA and FSA purposes, and tell patients to confirm with their plan administrator.[10] The IRS test is general. The expense must be primarily to alleviate or prevent a physical or mental defect or illness. Treatment at a health institute counts only if a physician prescribes it and states that it is necessary for that purpose.[26] Employer FSA plans can apply stricter rules than the IRS, and travel counts only when it is primarily for, and essential to, medical care.[27] Lodging can qualify only within limits. Get a letter from your own doctor before you go, confirm with your plan administrator in writing, and keep every receipt.

Payment, Deposits and Refunds

Expect to pay in full before treatment starts.[2] Regeneration Clinic says it offers no financing.[1] That contrasts with US clinics, which often offer CareCredit or in-house payment plans over 6 to 12 months.[10] Prices are in US dollars, the currency Panama uses.[5]

Deposit, refund and cancellation terms don't appear alongside the published prices, so ask for them in writing before you transfer money. Ask how much is due and when, which payment methods the clinic accepts and what fees apply, and what is refunded if you cancel or the on-site evaluation changes the plan.

Good to know · PAYING BY WIRE

A wire transfer is hard to reverse if a dispute comes up later. Make sure the itemized quote and the refund terms are signed before you send a large payment.

Flights from Miami

Copa advertises Miami flights from $317.[29] For actual dates, it listed round trips from $373 for October 22 to November 14, 2026.[30] It listed $535 for November 10 to 17, 2026.[31] Early January 2027 showed $582.[32] Summer 2027 dates ranged from $602 to $705.[33] Nonstop service is the norm on this route. Confirm that your booking is nonstop, because Copa also routes many flights through its Panama City hub.[34]

Hotel Nights

If your clinic doesn't include lodging, hotel rates swing widely from night to night. Hilton's own listing shows the Hilton Panama from $199 with a member discount.[35] Expedia showed $114 for November 9 to 10, 2026, while Travelocity showed $468 for November 29 to 30.[36][37] Stem Cell Institute patients get a standard Hilton room as part of treatment, so their main lodging cost is any extra nights.[9] Patients at other clinics can compare neighborhoods and hotels in our guide to where to stay in Panama City.

How Many Nights to Budget

The sources disagree on length of stay, and nights are the budget line you control. Auragens says most protocols run five days, Monday to Friday.[2] Regeneration Clinic says 3 to 7 working days, with a stay of no more than 10 days.[1] It adds that mild side effects mostly resolve within 24 hours, after which patients can fly home.[1] A medical tourism broker recommends staying 7 to 14 days after treatment.[38] Seven nights covers a five-day protocol with an arrival day and a buffer day. Ask your clinic how soon after the last session it clears you to fly.

Good to know · COMPANIONS

Auragens offers a companion program, and Stem Cell Institute suggests visiting the Panama Canal if time allows.[2][9] A companion with free afternoons can see the Miraflores Locks, about half a day from the hotel districts.

Questions

Before you go

How much does stem cell treatment cost in Panama?

Adult protocols run about $10,000 to $30,000 or more as of November 2026. Regeneration Clinic of Panama publishes $10,000 to $30,000 by protocol, and Auragens lists adult treatment from $28,750. Children's protocols at Auragens start at $14,500 for ages 12 and under.

What is the average cost of stem cell treatment?

There is no reliable average, because prices depend on what is being treated. A single joint injection in the US typically costs $3,000 to $10,000. Multi-day systemic protocols, like those Panama clinics sell, usually run $20,000 to $30,000 or more.

Is stem cell therapy cheaper in Panama than in the US?

Not per treatment, and the products differ. A US joint injection often costs less than a Panama protocol. Panama clinics sell multi-day, high-dose donor MSC infusions, which are not legally marketed in the US outside FDA-overseen trials. Compare written quotes for the same plan, not headline prices.

Is stem cell therapy cheaper in Panama or Mexico?

Mexico usually has the lower starting prices. Giostar in Cancún lists protocols from $6,900 to $18,968, and some Los Cabos clinics charge per injection over stays of several weeks. Panama is generally considered pricier than Mexico or Costa Rica, partly because packages bundle airport concierge, transfers and sometimes a hotel.

Are flights and hotel included in Panama stem cell packages?

Flights never are; the hotel depends on the clinic. Stem Cell Institute includes a standard Hilton Panama room, and Regeneration Clinic includes airport transport but not lodging. Meals and international flights are on you everywhere.

Does insurance cover stem cell therapy in Panama?

No. Panama clinics state that local and international insurance does not cover their treatments, and payment is usually due before treatment. Medicare covers stem cell transplants for blood-forming disorders, not regenerative injections or infusions abroad.

Can I use an HSA or FSA for stem cell therapy?

Possibly, but confirm with your plan first. The IRS requires the expense to treat a specific illness, and treatment at a health institute needs a physician's prescription and a statement that it is necessary. Employer FSA plans can be stricter, so get written confirmation from your administrator and keep a letter from your doctor.

How long do I need to stay in Panama for stem cell treatment?

Plan on about a week. Most protocols run three to seven working days, and Regeneration Clinic says stays are 10 days at most. Ask the clinic how soon after your last session it clears you to fly.

How much does stem cell therapy cost for autism in Panama?

Children's protocols start at about $14,500 at Auragens. Prices often scale with the child's weight, because cell doses do. See our guide to stem cell therapy for autism and MS in Panama for what clinics offer and what the research says.

Is stem cell therapy legal in Panama?

Clinics operate openly, but Panama's rules treat most uses as experimental. Under Executive Decree 179 of 2018, only blood-forming stem cell transplants have recognized evidence. Any other use is supposed to happen within protocols approved by the national bioethics committee and MINSA. Ask any clinic for its protocol approval details before paying.
