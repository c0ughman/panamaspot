Quick Questions

Before you book.

Do I need to be an experienced cyclist?
No. Most of our routes are flat or rolling and friendly for beginners. If you've ever ridden a bike, you're set. The mountain and India Dormida tours need a bit more leg — we'll match you to the right one.
Is it safe for kids and families?
Yes. We have child-friendly tours and bikes for younger riders. The town and waterfall loops are designed exactly for this.
What's actually included in the price?
The bike, a helmet, a lock, a front basket, and the GPS audio guide. No hidden fees. You just bring water and good shoes.
Where do I pick up the bike?
Plaza Paseo El Valle, on Avenida Principal — the main street of El Valle de Antón. Easy to find, easy parking.
Can I book for a group?
Absolutely. Send us the headcount on WhatsApp and we'll hold the bikes for your time slot.
What if it rains?
If the weather is dangerous to ride, we cancel and refund you in full — or reschedule for any day that works for you, no questions. Light rain doesn't stop us, but the call is yours: if you'd rather wait it out, we'll move the ride. Message us on WhatsApp the morning of and we'll sort it.
Do you speak English? Is the audio guide in English?
Yes to both. Our team is bilingual (English + Spanish), and the GPS audio guide narrates the whole tour in English.
