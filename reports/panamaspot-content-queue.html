Two rules that decide whether this works

Keep them to 2,500–4,000 words. Your own data argues against the 8,000-word default: things-to-do-el-valle is 8,606 words at position 30.7 and things-to-do-in-boquete is 8,315 at 40.4, while shorter named-attraction pages hold single digits. Specificity ranks, not length — and the shorter target roughly triples throughput.

Finish a cluster before opening another. El Valle ranks because it has sixteen pages and Google can read the topic as covered. Bocas fails on one. That is why batch 2 is six Bocas articles together rather than spread across the queue.

Do this first, before writing a word

Validate all 36 against DataForSEO search volume — about $3 and an hour. The evidence under each topic below is real GSC data from your own site, which proves people reach you on those themes. It does not prove the absolute volume is worth a page. Killing four topics that turn out to have no volume is cheaper than writing them, and it's the highest-return hour in this whole queue.

Scheduling this

Six batches of six. What that looks like against real throughput.

  • At 25 articles a month, all 36 land in about six weeks. That is roughly one solid article every working day — aggressive but the number the 4,200 clicks/month forecast assumes.
  • At 12 a month it is three months, and the forecast drops to something closer to 2,000–2,500 clicks. Still worth doing; just size the expectation to the pace you'll actually hold.
  • Batch 2 is the one not to break up. Six Bocas articles shipped together turn a dead cluster into a live one and make the Bocas hub buildable. Six Bocas articles spread over three months do considerably less.
  • New pages take 8–12 weeks to mature. Your own July batch is the evidence — impressions kept climbing through August on content published in mid-July. Nothing you publish this week shows its real position until November.
  • After each batch, re-run scripts/chrome-fix.py and rebuild the hubs. Both are idempotent and pick up new articles automatically, so internal linking and the footer stay current without anyone remembering to do it.
