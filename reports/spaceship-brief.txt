WHAT THIS IS
36 article briefs, batched. Each maps to one SeoTopic row: `topic`,
`primary_keyword`, `secondary_keywords`, `notes`. The suggested slug is included
because the pipeline derives slugs from primary_keyword and these need to differ
in a few cases.

BEFORE QUEUEING ANYTHING — FOUR SETUP ITEMS

1. TWO SeoSite ROWS ARE NEEDED, NOT ONE.
   `language`, `serp_language_code` and `serp_location_code` live on SeoSite, not
   on SeoTopic. 23 of these are English and 13 are Spanish, and the Spanish ones
   target Panama, not the US. So:
     - panamaspot-en : language "en", serp_language_code "en", pages_directory "public/articles/"
     - panamaspot-es : language "es", serp_language_code "es", pages_directory "public/es/articles/"
   Both should use the Panama SERP location, not the 2840 (United States) default
   — 232 of the site's 397 clicks come from Panama. Verify the DataForSEO code for
   Panama before the first run rather than trusting a remembered value.

2. CREDENTIALS. The pipeline needs TAVILY_API_KEY (research stage) and
   GITHUB_TOKEN (publish stage). Neither is in the local backend/.env. Confirm
   both are set on Railway or the run dies at stage 4. DataForSEO is funded
   ($41.82 as of 31 Aug).

3. VALIDATE VOLUME FIRST — about $3 and one hour. The keywords below come from
   the site's own Search Console data, which proves people reach this domain on
   these themes. It does NOT prove the absolute volume justifies a page. Pull
   DataForSEO search volume for all 36 primary keywords and drop anything with no
   real demand before writing.

4. PAGES ARE STATIC HTML IN public/, NOT NEXT ROUTES. Existing articles live at
   public/articles/*.html and public/es/articles/*.html. The template extractor
   is spaceship/backend/extract_ps_template.py. Do not create src/app routes.

HOUSE RULES — APPLY TO ALL 36

LENGTH: 2,500-4,000 words. Do NOT default to 8,000. The site's own data argues
  against it: things-to-do-el-valle is 8,606 words and ranks 30.7;
  things-to-do-in-boquete is 8,315 and ranks 40.4; shorter named-attraction pages
  hold single digits. Specificity ranks, not length.

TITLES: 60 rendered characters maximum, keyword first, then one specific
  promise — a price, a distance, a duration, a count. Not "Complete Guide".
  Working examples now live on the site:
     India Dormida Hike: 3.5 km, 2-3 Hours, $3 Entry
     Miraflores Locks 2026: $17.22 Entry & When Ships Pass
     33 Things to Do in El Valle de Anton (2026)
  Count the rendered character, not the HTML entity: "&amp;" is one character.

DESCRIPTIONS: 160 rendered characters maximum. Lead with the concrete fact and
  stop before the payoff.

FACTS: every price, distance and duration must be verifiable and dated. Where a
  figure could not be confirmed, write around it rather than guessing. Two
  existing pages currently contradict each other on the Caldera hot springs entry
  fee ($7 on the English page, $3-5 on the Spanish) — do not propagate either
  number into new pages until it is resolved.

SPANISH: native Panamanian Spanish, not translated English. The Spanish articles
  are NOT translations of the English ones and must not be written as such — the
  two libraries deliberately cover different ground.

SCHEMA: Article + FAQPage + BreadcrumbList, matching the existing pages. 10-12
  FAQs per article. Check the target page's existing FAQs before adding one — on
  the current site, eleven of twelve "missing" questions turned out to be already
  answered under different wording.

INTERNAL LINKS: 10-14 per article. Every article links up to its destination hub
  (/articles/el-valle-de-anton, /articles/boquete, /articles/panama-city and the
  three /es/ equivalents) and across to 3-4 siblings in the same cluster.

CLIENT CTA: El Valle and Boquete articles carry an E-Valley Bikes CTA pointing at
  /funnels/evalley_elvalle or /funnels/evalley_boquete (append _es on Spanish
  pages). Panama City and Bocas articles carry NO CTA — there is no client there.
  scripts/cta-pass.py handles injection; do not hand-write these blocks.

AFTER EACH BATCH, run in the panamaspot repo:
     python3 scripts/chrome-fix.py       # footer + Spanish header, idempotent
     python3 scripts/build-hub.py        # hubs pick up new articles
     npm run build                       # asserts analytics on every page
